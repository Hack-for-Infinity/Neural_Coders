The Foundation

Four Pillars of Accessible Learning

Every EduVoice feature traces back to one of these pillars. Together they ensure no learner is excluded by design.

  • A

    Auditory

    Every lesson is available as audio. Read-aloud, text-to-speech, and podcast-style versions mean screen reader and visually impaired students lose nothing.

  • V

    Visual

    Adjustable text size, dyslexia-friendly fonts, high-contrast modes, and captioned video. Every visual choice is a learner's choice, not a designer's constraint.

  • I

    Interactive

    Quizzes and assessments fully operable by keyboard and screen reader. Voice answers and simplified structures to reduce cognitive overload.

  • D

    Dyslexia-First

    Lexend font options, generous line spacing, short paragraphs, and never relying on colour alone to convey meaning. Readable by design.

What We Guarantee

The EduVoice Accessibility Checklist

Every lesson, quiz, and video is verified against these criteria before it goes live.

  • ✓
    Audio Alternative on Every LessonEvery written lesson has a read-aloud version. No content is text-only.
  • ✓
    Indian Sign Language SupportISL shown alongside read-aloud for documents, images, videos, and captions.
  • ✓
    Captions on All VideoEvery lecture ships with synchronised captions and ISL support.
  • ✓
    Keyboard-Only NavigationEvery quiz, player, and control is fully operable without a mouse.
  • ✓
    Screen Reader AnnouncementsResults, progress, and errors are announced via ARIA live regions — not just shown visually.
  • ✓
    Reduced Cognitive OverloadFocus mode, simplified layouts, one-thing-at-a-time quiz structure, and clear labelling throughout.
  • ✓
    Dyslexia Font AvailableLexend toggle available globally and in the Learning Lab. Applies to uploaded documents too.
  • ✓
    4.5:1 Colour ContrastAll text and UI elements meet WCAG AA contrast minimums. No grey text on white backgrounds.
